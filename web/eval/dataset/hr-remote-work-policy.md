# Remote Work Policy (Excerpt)

**Policy ID:** HR-014
**Effective:** January 1, 2025
**Owner:** People Operations, Northwind Analytics Inc.
**Approver:** Sofia Duarte, Chief People Officer

## 1. Eligibility

All full-time employees who have completed the ninety (90) day onboarding period are eligible
for hybrid work. Employees in the Support organization are excluded while on an on-call
rotation. Contractors are not covered by this policy; their arrangements are governed by their
individual statements of work.

## 2. Expectations

Hybrid employees are required to be present in their assigned office at least two (2) days per
week. The assigned office is recorded in Workday and may only be changed with approval from the
employee's manager and People Operations.

## 3. Equipment

Northwind Analytics Inc. provides a laptop and a monitor stipend of USD 500 per employee, valid
once every three (3) years. Equipment remains the property of Northwind Analytics Inc. and must
be returned on termination.

## 4. Relocation

Employees who relocate to a different tax jurisdiction must notify People Operations at least
sixty (60) days in advance. Relocations outside a country where Northwind Analytics Inc. has a
registered entity will not be approved.

## 5. Exceptions

Exceptions require written approval from Sofia Duarte. Questions should be directed to People
Operations.
