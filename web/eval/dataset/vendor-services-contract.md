# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of March 14, 2025 (the
"Effective Date") between Northwind Analytics Inc., a Delaware corporation ("Client"), and
Brightpath Consulting LLC, a New York limited liability company ("Provider").

## 1. Services

Provider shall deliver data platform migration services to Client as described in Statement of
Work No. 1 ("SOW-1"), attached hereto. SOW-1 covers migration of Client's reporting warehouse
to a managed cloud environment.

## 2. Term and Termination

The Agreement is effective on the Effective Date and continues for twenty-four (24) months
unless terminated earlier. Either party may terminate for convenience on ninety (90) days
written notice. Client may terminate immediately upon a material breach by Provider that
remains uncured for thirty (30) days after notice.

## 3. Fees

Client shall pay Provider a fixed fee of USD 480,000, invoiced monthly in arrears. Late
payments accrue interest at 1.5% per month.

## 4. Confidentiality

Each party is obligated to protect the other party's Confidential Information for a period of
five (5) years following termination.

## 5. Indemnification

Provider indemnifies Client against any third-party claim arising from Provider's infringement
of intellectual property rights.

## 6. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware. Disputes are resolved by
binding arbitration administered by the American Arbitration Association in Wilmington,
Delaware.

Signed by Dana Whitfield, General Counsel of Northwind Analytics Inc., and by Marcus Ellery,
Managing Partner of Brightpath Consulting LLC.
