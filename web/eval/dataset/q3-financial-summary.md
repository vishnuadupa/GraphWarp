# Northwind Analytics Inc. — Q3 FY2025 Financial Summary

Northwind Analytics Inc. (NASDAQ: NWND) reported third-quarter revenue of USD 62.4 million, up
18% year over year. Gross margin improved to 71%, and the company recorded net income of USD
4.1 million, its second consecutive profitable quarter.

## Segment Performance

The Platform segment generated USD 48.9 million, driven by adoption of the Atlas Reporting
Suite. The Services segment contributed USD 13.5 million, a decline of 4% attributed to the
wind-down of legacy implementation contracts.

## Corporate Development

On August 6, 2025, Northwind acquired Lumen Metrics Ltd., a UK-based data quality vendor, for
USD 27 million in cash. Lumen Metrics now operates as a wholly owned subsidiary. Northwind also
increased its stake in Corvus Data Systems from 12% to 19%.

## Leadership and Governance

Chief Executive Officer Priya Raghavan noted that the company continues to compete with Delta
Insight Corp. in the mid-market segment. Chief Financial Officer Tom Beckett confirmed that
full-year guidance remains at USD 250 million. The audit is performed by Harkness & Wolfe LLP.
Northwind is regulated by the Securities and Exchange Commission.

## Outlook

Management expects Q4 revenue between USD 66 million and USD 69 million, with continued margin
expansion from the Atlas Reporting Suite.
